Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9): one line, at most 160 characters, roasting the smart fridge.

> My smart fridge locked the freezer during a firmware update and held my ice cream hostage until I agreed to the new terms.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it against the gate (1–160 characters, one line, not only whitespace) before and after writing. Run with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; outputs are byte-identical across runs.
